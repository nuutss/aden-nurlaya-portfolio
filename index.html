CASE 01 • SCIENTIFIC INQUIRY

The Impact of FOMO on Deviant Behavior Among High School Students

May – June 2025

01 / The Problem

Adolescent peer circles are increasingly hijacked by social-media induced Fear of Missing Out (FOMO). Students regularly succumb to negative conformity and hazardous behaviors simply to maintain superficial validation within their peer groups.

02 / The Research Process

Formulated empirical hypotheses and conducted in-depth qualitative one-on-one interviews across varied student social clusters at SMAN 1 Sumedang, transcribing and coding narratives to uncover the root psychosocial motivations of teen deviance.

03 / The Product & Concrete Impact

Produced a formal Scientific Research Paper (Karya Tulis Ilmiah). Distinctively, the primary recommendations were addressed directly to the family and household environment rather than conventional punitive school measures, identifying parental awareness as the foremost proactive safeguard.

04 / Personal Reflection

"FOMO misdirected into peer-pleasing can derail adolescent potential. My deepest takeaway is that FOMO must be handled with conscious intentionality: channel the drive to participate solely toward deliberate, positive growth rather than impulsive trends."

CASE 02 • SOFTWARE & HARDWARE INTEGRATION

DiPay — Contactless Mobile Payment Ecosystem (NFC Tap-to-Pay)

Kotlin • Node.js • NFC HCE

01 / The Problem

Physical cash dependence in high-density campus environments leads to bottleneck queues, transaction delays, and reconciliation inaccuracies across student cafeterias and school co-ops.

02 / Engineering Process

Engineered an Android mobile application leveraging NFC Host Card Emulation (HCE) to mimic contactless smart cards, connected to a dedicated Node.js backend server verifying cryptographic tokens and account balances in sub-second roundtrips.

03 / Product & Practical Impact

Successfully deployed the complete executable APK (DiPay.apk) with backend integration, validating an ultra-fast, offline-capable tap-to-pay transaction flow suitable for localized academic ecosystems.

04 / Personal Reflection

"Speed and security in IoT protocols must be architected simultaneously. Building DiPay proved that convenience must never compromise data integrity."
